# Parts Bin

A free, open-source inventory app for makers and electronics hobbyists.

Tired of digging through drawers wondering if you still have any 10k
resistors? Parts Bin keeps track of everything on your bench — components,
3D-printer filament, PCBs, screws, whole projects, incoming orders, and
even which physical drawer each part lives in.

It runs right on your own PC in its own window. No account, no sign-up,
no internet required, and nothing is ever uploaded — all your data stays
on your computer.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Track components, filament, PCBs & screws — with photos, prices and
  total stock value
- Get low-stock and out-of-stock warnings at a glance
- Buy in bulk? Track whole packs AND exact units
- Print barcode labels (small/medium/large) and scan them with your camera
- Plan projects with photos, checklists and the exact parts they use
- Track incoming orders that add themselves to your stock when they arrive
- Map your live storage — shelves and drawer cabinets — and see exactly
  where any part is kept
- Make it yours: lots of light/dark/gradient themes, a custom accent
  colour, font choices, and an optional PIN lock
- Back up and restore your whole inventory to a file anytime

HOW TO INSTALL (Windows)
1. Download the ZIP and unzip it
2. Open the PartsBin-Setup folder
3. Double-click "Install Parts Bin.bat"
   (if Windows warns, click More info → Run anyway — it's a safe free script)
4. Click the new "Parts Bin" icon on your Desktop
