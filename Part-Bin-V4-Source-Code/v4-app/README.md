Parts Bin
A free, open source inventory app for makers and electronics hobbyists.
Tired of digging through drawers wondering if you still have any 10k resistors? Parts Bin keeps track of everthing on your bench. Components, 3D printer filament, PCBs, screws, whole projects, incoming orders, and even which physical drawer each part lives in.
It runs right on your own PC in its own window. No account, no sign up, no internet required, and nothing is ever uploaded. All your data stays on your computer.
Features

Track components, filament, PCBs and screws with photos, prices and total stock value
Low stock and out of stock warnings at a glance
Buy in bulk? Track whole packs AND exact units
Orders with multiple products in one order, mark it "arrived" and everything gets added to your stock at once
Print barcode labels (small, medium or large) and scan them with your camera
Scan a part to instantly see what it is and where its stored
Plan projects with photos, checklists and the exact parts they use
Map your real storage like shelves and drawer cabinets, and link parts to drawers
Archive finished projects and arrived orders (with optional auto archiving)
Make it yours with lots of light, dark and gradient themes, a custom accent colour and font choices
Optional 4 digit PIN lock
Back up and restore your whole inventory to a file anytime

How to install (Windows)

Download the ZIP and unzip it (right click then Extract All).
Open the PartsBin-Setup folder.
Double click "Install Parts Bin.bat".
Click the new "Parts Bin" icon on your Desktop.

Thats it. The app installs to your PC and opens in its own window.
A note on the Windows warning
When you run the installer, Windows might show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box that says the publisher is unknown or unverified.
This is completly normal and safe. It shows up for any free app that hasnt been "code signed" (a paid certificate that small free projects skip). It does not mean anything is wrong with the app.
To run it:

Click "More info" on the blue box.
Click "Run anyway".

You only need to do this once. Tip: if a downloaded file seems blocked, right click it, go to Properties, tick "Unblock" then OK (do this on the zip before extracting).
Your data and privacy
Everything you add stays on your own computer in your browsers local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere and there is no account.
To move your data to another PC or keep a safe copy, open the app and go to Settings then Backup to file. You can restore it later from the same screen.
Versions
Each release is uploaded as its own ZIP (Parts Bin V1, Parts Bin V2 and so on) so you can always grab the newest one, or an older one if you prefer.
Removing it
Run "Uninstall Parts Bin.bat" to remove the app and its shortcut. Back up your data first if you want to keep it.
